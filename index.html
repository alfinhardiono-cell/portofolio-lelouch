About Me

Curious about how technology connects.

I'm a vocational high school student majoring in Computer and Network Engineering. What started as curiosity about how computers talk to each other grew into a love for building things people can actually use.

On the web side, I build responsive interfaces with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. On the infrastructure side, I design and troubleshoot networks, and I like experimenting with Arduino to connect software to the physical world.

I believe in learning by doing. Every project here taught me something new, and I'm always looking for the next challenge.
